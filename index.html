Phase 1: Business Understanding (Boston Housing)

Hedonic Real Estate Pricing: Quantifying the marginal willingness to pay for home size, neighborhood amenities, school quality, and environmental health across 506 Boston census tracts.

1
Property Appraisal & Underwriting

Provides institutional lenders, assessors, and home buyers with an objective, data-driven hedonic benchmark of tract-level real estate values.

2
Urban Economics & Environmental Penalties

Quantifies the negative price externalities of air pollution (nitric oxide NOX) and crime (CRIM) against residential capital.

3
Occam's Razor in Urban Modeling

Screens 13 demographic, geographical, and structural candidates to eliminate collinearity (e.g., redundant age and tax factors).

Target Variable

MEDV ($1,000s)

13 Predictor Categories

  • Structural: RM, AGE
  • Demographic: LSTAT, PTRATIO, B
  • Environmental: NOX, DIS, CHAS
  • Economic: TAX, RAD, CRIM, ZN, INDUS
